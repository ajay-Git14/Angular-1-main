# Topic 1: Dependency Injection

## Goal

Understand Dependency Injection (DI), learn how Angular creates and manages objects automatically, and understand why DI is one of the core architectural foundations of Angular.

---

# What

Dependency Injection is a design pattern used to provide required objects (dependencies) to a class instead of creating them manually.

Without Dependency Injection:

```text
Component
    ↓
Creates Service
    ↓
Uses Service
```

With Dependency Injection:

```text
Angular Injector
        ↓
Creates Service
        ↓
Provides Service
        ↓
Component Uses Service
```

---

# Real-Life Analogy

Imagine a restaurant.

Without DI:

```text
Customer
     ↓
Goes To Kitchen
     ↓
Cooks Food
     ↓
Eats Food
```

Not practical.

---

With DI:

```text
Customer
     ↓
Waiter
     ↓
Kitchen
     ↓
Food Served
```

Customer only consumes food.

Similarly:

```text
Component
      ↓
Uses Service

Angular
      ↓
Creates Service
```

# Why

Without Dependency Injection:

```text
Tightly Coupled Code

```

---

With Dependency Injection:

```text

Reusability

Testability

Maintainability

Scalability
```

# Understanding Dependency

## What Is Dependency?

A dependency is anything a class needs to perform its work.

Example:

```text
Complaint Component
        ↓
Needs Complaint Service
```

Here:

```text
Complaint Service
```

is a dependency.

# Constructor Injection vs inject()

| Constructor       | inject()                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------- |
| Traditional       | Modern                    |
| Constructor Based | Function Based            |
