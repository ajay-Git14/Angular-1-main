## Goal

Understand Child Routes in Angular, learn how to create nested navigation structures, and build enterprise applications with dashboards, admin panels, and multi-level navigation.

---

# What

Child Routes are routes nested inside another route.

Without Child Routes:

```text
Dashboard
     ↓
Separate Routes
```

With Child Routes:

```text
Dashboard
    ├── Complaints
    ├── Residents
    ├── Reports
    └── Settings
```

---

# Real-Life Analogy

Imagine a shopping mall.

```text
Mall
 ├── Electronics
 ├── Clothing
 ├── Food Court
 └── Cinema
```

The mall is the parent.

Individual sections are children.

Similarly:

```text
Dashboard
     ↓
Child Screens
```

---

# Why

Without Child Routes:

Flat Navigation

With Child Routes:
