# Topic 1: Forms

## Goal

Understand Forms in Angular, learn why forms are essential in web applications, and understand how Angular collects, manages, and processes user data.

---

# What

A Form is a mechanism used to collect information from users.

Examples:

```text id="tk8lxq"
Login Form

Registration Form

Contact Form

Complaint Form

Feedback Form

Payment Form
```

In Angular:

```text id="c4wgx4"
User Input
      ↓
Angular Form
      ↓
Validation
      ↓
Business Logic
      ↓
Database/API
```

---

# Real-Life Analogy

Imagine a Hospital Registration Desk.

Patient provides:

```text id="z7v7zw"
Name

Age

Mobile Number

Address

Symptoms
```

Receptionist collects data.

Similarly:

```text id="mw7g5s"
User
    ↓
Form
    ↓
Application
```
